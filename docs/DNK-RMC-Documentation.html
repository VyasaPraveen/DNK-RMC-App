Section 01

Overview & access

The DNK RMC system replaces paper registers and spreadsheets with one web application that every authorised staff member can open from any device. Everything — bills, payments, stock, attendance and reports — lives in one place and stays in sync automatically.

What the system does

It handles the full cycle of a concrete plant: raise a GST-correct tax invoice for each dispatch, track who owes money, manage raw-material stock and vendor purchases, run staff attendance and salary, and hand your accountant ready-made GST reports. It works online across all your computers and phones, and keeps working even if the internet drops.

🌐

How to open it

Open any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari) on a computer or phone and go to the address below. No installation needed.

Address

https://dnk-rmc.web.app

🧩

How it's organised

A menu on the left groups every screen into five areas so you always know where to look:

  • Operations — dashboard, billing, outstanding
  • Plant & Staff — stock, vendors, attendance, payroll
  • Sales Tools — leads & calculators
  • Masters — customers, grades, rates
  • Insights — reports, users, settings
Section 02

Logging in & user roles

Each person signs in with their own username and password. What they can see and change depends on their role, so the right people get the right access.

🔑

Signing in

How to use
  1. Open dnk-rmc.web.app — the login screen appears.
  2. Enter your username (or email) and password, then press Enter or click Sign In.
  3. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password? and answer your security question to reset it.
  4. Use the eye icon in the password box to check what you typed.

The three roles

RoleUsernameWhat they can do
AdminadminFull access — every module, plus user management, settings, no-code customisation and feature toggles.
AccountantaccountantDay-to-day billing and operations. Cannot open admin-only settings.
AuditorauditorRead-only — can view and download everything but cannot edit or delete.
Change your password first Your initial passwords were provided separately by EPIXS Media. For security, sign in and change the Admin password immediately, and don't share the Admin login. Roles and passwords are managed in Users & Permissions.
Section 03

Dashboard

The first screen after login. It gives you the day's picture at a glance so you know exactly where things stand before doing anything else.

📊

What you see

Live figures that update as you work:

  • Today's dispatch — total cubic metres billed today.

Section 04

Billing & invoices

The heart of the system. Raise a GST-correct tax invoice for every concrete dispatch, keep them all searchable, and track exactly who still owes money.

➕

New Dispatch / Bill

Creates a tax invoice. GST is worked out automatically from the customer's GSTIN and state — you never calculate tax by hand.

How to use
  1. Go to Operations → New Dispatch / Bill.
  2. Pick the Customer. The system detects the tax type: CGST + SGST within Andhra Pradesh, IGST for other states, or No GST for an unregistered buyer.
  3. Choose the Site/Project (optional), Concrete Grade, and Vehicle & Driver.
  4. Enter Quantity (Cum) and Rate — the rate auto-fills if set in the Rate Master. (For a PUMP grade these two are optional.)
  5. Optional: add Pump Charges and tick Apply GST on pump if they're taxable.
  6. Check the live Invoice Summary on the right — taxable value, GST and grand total, plus the amount in words.
  7. Click Generate Invoice & PDF. The invoice opens ready to print or save as PDF, and is stored under Invoices.
Good to know Invoice numbers are assigned automatically (DNK/1, DNK/2 …) and stay fixed for registered buyers. For unregistered buyers the number is editable if you need to match a manual book.
🧾

Invoices

Every bill you've raised, in one searchable list.

How to use
  1. Open Operations → Invoices.
  2. Filter by search, customer, or a date range (From / To).
  3. Use the row actions to edit, delete, print the Tax Invoice or Batch Slip, or a customer Statement.
  4. Export the whole list to Excel/CSV from the row or from Reports.
💰

Outstanding

Track pending bills and record collections.

How to use
  1. Open Operations → Outstanding.
  2. Find the bill under Pending Bills.
  3. Record a payment — amount, mode and date.
  4. The balance updates instantly and the entry appears, time-stamped, under Recent Payments.
Section 05

Plant & staff

Everything behind the invoice — raw materials, vendors, vehicles, and the people who run the plant.

📦

Product Inventory

A stock register for cement, aggregates, sand, admixtures and more.

How to use
  1. Open Plant & Staff → Product Inventory.
  2. Add a product with its opening stock, unit and reorder level.
  3. Recording a vendor purchase adds stock automatically; use Stock Out for usage.
  4. Items at or below reorder show a red LOW tag and a dashboard alert.
🚛

Vendors & Purchases

Supplier records and every purchase you make.

How to use
  1. Open Plant & Staff → Vendors & Purchases.
  2. Add a vendor (name, GSTIN, phone).
  3. Record a purchase and link it to a product to top up stock.
  4. Filter purchases by date range and export.
🧱

Materials Received

An inward log of materials arriving at the plant.

How to use
  1. Open Plant & Staff → Materials Received.
  2. Add an entry — date, material type, quantity, note.
  3. Print or export the summary when needed.
🛢️

Vehicle Log

Track running and fuel for each transit mixer / tipper.

How to use
  1. Open Plant & Staff → Vehicle Log.
  2. Enter the opening and closing meter readings and fuel.
  3. Distance is calculated for you; export any time.
👷

Staff Attendance & Payroll

Mark daily attendance and let the system work out salary from it.

How to use
  1. Open Plant & Staff → Staff Attendance. For each person mark P (present), A (absent), H (half-day) or L (leave) — or use Mark All Present. Future dates and pre-joining dates are blocked automatically.
  2. Open Salary / Payroll. Pay is calculated from attendance: Absent = unpaid, and Leave = paid up to the monthly allowance (only extra leave becomes loss-of-pay).
  3. Record advances — you're warned if an advance exceeds the salary due.
  4. Print a payslip for any employee.

Section 07

Master data

Set these up once and the rest of the system fills itself in. Anything you enter here appears in the dropdowns on the billing screens.

🗂️

The five masters

  • Customers — name, GSTIN (validated), state and address. Drives automatic GST.
  • Sites / Projects — delivery sites linked to customers.
  • Vehicles & Drivers — transit mixers, tippers and drivers.
  • Concrete Grades — M10, M20, M25 … each with its HSN/SAC and mix design.
  • Rate Master — the agreed rate per customer × grade, so bills price themselves.
How to use
  1. Open the item under Masters.
  2. Click Add, fill the form, and save. GSTIN and phone numbers are checked for you.
  3. Edit or remove any record from its row.
Section 08

Reports & GST

Turn your data into the files your accountant and tax filing need — in the exact formats they expect.

📈

Reports & Export

All exports live in one place under Insights → Reports & Export.

What you can download
  • All Invoices (Excel/CSV) — with separate CGST, SGST and IGST columns, so it works for both intra- and inter-state tax.
  • Monthly Summary — bills, quantity, taxable, GST and total per month.
  • GSTR Sales Register (Tally) — pick a month; downloads in Tally's exact layout (D-Mon-YY dates, Dr/Cr, Grand Total) so your accountant can use it directly.
  • Toll / Dispatch Register and Bulk Invoice ZIP for a whole customer or period.
How to export the GST register
  1. Open Reports & Export.
  2. Choose the month next to GST Register.
  3. Click GSTR Sales Register (Tally) — the file downloads ready to hand over.
Section 09

Admin & no-code setup

The Admin can shape the system without any programming — add options, turn modules on or off, manage users, import history and back everything up. All of this lives in Settings & Backup and Users & Permissions.

🧩

Manage Lists & Options — no coding

Change the choices that appear in dropdowns across the app yourself. Great when you add a new material or a new lead stage.

How to use
  1. Open Settings → Manage Lists & Options.
  2. Pick a list — Dispatched Through, Product Categories, Units, Material Types or Lead Stages.
  3. Add, rename or remove options; Reset to default restores the originals.
  4. Changes apply instantly and sync to every device. Existing records keep their saved value even if you later remove an option.
🎚️

Feature Management

Show or hide whole modules for everyone.

How to use
  1. Open Settings → Feature Management.
  2. Toggle any module on or off — it appears or disappears from every user's menu at once (core screens stay on).
👥

Users & Permissions

Create logins and decide exactly what each can touch.

How to use
  1. Open Insights → Users & Permissions.
  2. Add a user, choose a role, and tick the modules they may use.
  3. Disable a login any time without deleting its history.
⬆️

Import your history (April 2026 onward)

Bring older records in from a spreadsheet instead of retyping them.

How to use
  1. Open Settings → Import Data.
  2. Choose a module — Invoices/Sales, Materials, Vehicle Log, Customers, Vendors, Staff or Products.
  3. Click Download Template, fill it in, and save as CSV or Excel (.xlsx).
  4. Choose your file and click Import. Customers, vendors, grades and vehicles are created for you, GST on invoices is calculated automatically, and duplicates are skipped.
Tip CSV files import fully offline; Excel (.xlsx) import needs an internet connection at the moment you import.
💾

Backup & company details

How to use
  1. Open Settings & Backup.
  2. Edit Company Details (name, GSTIN, address, bank) — these print on every invoice.
  3. Click Download Backup (.json) to save a full copy; Restore brings it back on any computer.
Recommended Download a backup weekly and keep it somewhere safe, in addition to the automatic cloud sync.
Section 10

Sync, security & mobile

The system is built to be safe, always up to date, and usable from the plant on a phone.

☁️

Real-time cloud sync

Log in on two computers and both stay in step — a bill raised on one appears on the other within seconds. A small badge shows Synced, Connecting or Local only. If the internet drops, the app keeps working and syncs again when it returns.

🔒

Security

Sign-in required, role-based access, validated GST and phone entries, and hardening against malicious data and scripts (Content-Security-Policy and input escaping). Invoices print clean on A4 with no browser clutter.

📱

Mobile ready

On a phone the menu becomes a slide-in drawer (tap ☰), wide tables scroll neatly, and forms stack to one column — so you can raise a bill or check outstanding from the site.

⚡

Fast & lightweight

The whole app is a single lightweight page that loads quickly and puts almost no load on the server, so it stays responsive even on modest connections.
